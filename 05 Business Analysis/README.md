# Business Analysis

This folder contains the detailed business analysis performed for the WealthBridge Investment Preferences project.

The analysis addresses six key business questions designed to help WealthBridge Advisors better understand investor demographics, investment preferences, decision-making behavior, and savings objectives.

Each question is supported by quantitative analysis, business insights, and visualizations derived from the Power BI dashboard.

---

## Questions Covered

- Investment Preferences by Gender
- Investment Preferences by Age Group
- Investment Decision Factors by Age Group
- Savings Objectives by Investment Duration
- Mutual Fund Investment Reasons by Expected Returns
- Executive KPIs and Investment Trends

The document includes:

- Business interpretations
- Actionable recommendations
