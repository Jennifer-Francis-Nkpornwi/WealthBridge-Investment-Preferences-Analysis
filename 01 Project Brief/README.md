# Project Brief

## Project Title
**Understanding Investment Preferences and Investor Behavior**

| **Client** | WealthBridge Advisors |
|------------|-----------------------|
| **Industry** | Financial Services |
| **Role** | Data Analyst |

---

# Business Background

WealthBridge Advisors is a financial advisory firm that helps individuals make informed investment decisions through products such as Mutual Funds, Equity Investments, Government Bonds, Fixed Deposits, Public Provident Funds (PPF), and other wealth management solutions.

As the firm continues to expand its client base, it aims to better understand investor behavior and the factors influencing investment decisions. Management wants to identify the investment products preferred by different customer segments, understand the motivations behind those choices, and uncover demographic trends that can support more targeted marketing and advisory services.

To support this initiative, WealthBridge Advisors conducted an investor survey capturing demographic information, investment preferences, savings objectives, expected returns, and key decision-making factors.

The management team requires a comprehensive analysis of this data to generate actionable insights that will improve customer segmentation, product positioning, and strategic decision-making.

---

# Project Objective

Analyze investor survey data to identify patterns in investment behavior and provide business insights that help WealthBridge Advisors:

- Understand investor demographics and customer segments.
- Identify the most preferred investment avenues.
- Determine how investment preferences vary across gender and age groups.
- Understand the factors influencing investment decisions.
- Analyze how savings objectives change across investment durations.
- Explore the relationship between expected returns and reasons for investing in Mutual Funds.
- Develop executive KPIs for monitoring investment trends and customer behavior.

---

# Dataset Overview

The dataset consists of survey responses from individual investors.

Each record represents one respondent and contains information including:

- Demographic details
- Preferred investment avenues
- Investment duration
- Expected returns
- Savings objectives
- Investment decision factors
- Sources of investment information

---

# Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer the following business questions.

## 1. Investment Preferences by Gender

Analyze the preferred investment avenues (Mutual Funds, Equity Market, Debentures, Government Bonds, Fixed Deposits, PPF, and Gold) across male and female investors.

Determine:

- The most preferred investment avenue for each gender.
- Key differences in investment preferences between male and female investors.

---

## 2. Investment Preferences by Age Group

Segment respondents into the following age groups:

- **Early Adulthood:** 21–25 years
- **Established Adulthood:** 26–30 years
- **Experienced Adulthood:** 31–35 years

Determine:

- The number of investors within each age group.
- Their preferred investment avenues.

---

## 3. Investment Decision Factors

Identify the primary factors influencing investment decisions across different age groups.

Examples include:

- Returns
- Risk
- Lock-in Period
- Other investment considerations

Determine which factors have the greatest influence within each age segment.

---

## 4. Savings Objectives by Investment Duration

Analyze the relationship between investment duration and investors' primary savings objectives.

### Investment Durations

- Less than 1 year
- 1–3 years
- 3–5 years
- More than 5 years

### Savings Objectives

- Retirement Planning
- Health Care
- Education
- Other financial goals

Identify how investor priorities change across different investment horizons.

---

## 5. Mutual Fund Investment Behavior

Analyze the reasons investors choose Mutual Funds based on their expected return ranges:

- 10–20%
- 20–30%
- 30–40%

Determine which return expectations are most strongly associated with motivations such as:

- Better Returns
- Fund Diversification
- Tax Benefits
- Other investment considerations

---

## 6. Executive KPIs

Develop a set of business KPIs that provide management with an overview of investor behavior and investment trends.

Suggested KPIs include:

- Total Respondents
- Average Respondent Age
- Most Preferred Investment Avenue
- Top Investment Decision Factor
- Leading Savings Objective

---

# Expected Deliverables

The project will deliver the following outputs:

- ✅ Cleaned and validated dataset for analysis
- ✅ Interactive Power BI dashboard
- ✅ Business analysis report addressing all six business questions
- ✅ Executive summary highlighting key findings, business implications, and strategic recommendations

---

# Expected Business Outcome

The insights generated from this analysis will enable WealthBridge Advisors to:

- Better understand its target audience.
- Improve customer segmentation strategies.
- Align investment products with customer preferences.
- Personalize financial advisory services.
