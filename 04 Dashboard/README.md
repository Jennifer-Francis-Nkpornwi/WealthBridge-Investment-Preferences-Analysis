# Interactive Dashboard

This folder contains the Power BI dashboard developed for the **WealthBridge Investment Preferences Analysis** project.

The dashboard transforms investor survey data into interactive visualizations that enable stakeholders to quickly understand investment behavior, customer segments, and the factors influencing investment decisions.

# Dashboard Objectives

The dashboard was designed to help WealthBridge Advisors answer key business questions by enabling users to:

- Compare investment preferences across demographic groups.
- Identify the most preferred investment avenues.
- Understand the factors driving investment decisions.
- Analyze savings objectives based on investment duration.
- Explore the motivations behind Mutual Fund investments.
- Monitor executive KPIs for investor behavior.

---

# Dashboard Features

### Investment Preferences by Gender

Compares investment avenues preferred by male and female investors to identify differences in investment behavior.

**Business Value**

Supports targeted product positioning and customer segmentation.

---

### Investment Preferences by Age Group

Shows how investment preferences vary across different age segments.

**Business Value**

Helps identify the firm's most valuable customer segments and tailor investment offerings accordingly.

---

### Investment Decision Factors

Analyzes the factors influencing investment decisions across age groups.

Examples include:

- Returns
- Risk
- Lock-in Period

**Business Value**

Provides insight into what matters most to different investor segments.

---

### Savings Objectives by Investment Duration

Explores how savings goals change depending on investment time horizons.

Examples include:

- Retirement Planning
- Health Care
- Education

**Business Value**

Supports the development of investment products aligned with customers' long-term financial goals.

---

### Reasons for Investing in Mutual Funds

Examines how expected returns influence the reasons investors choose Mutual Funds.

**Business Value**

Helps position Mutual Funds based on investor expectations and motivations.

---

# Executive KPIs

The dashboard includes management KPIs to provide an at-a-glance view of investor trends.

- 👥 Total Respondents
- 🎂 Average Respondent Age
- 💹 Preferred Investment Avenue
- 📈 Top Investment Factor
- 🎯 Leading Savings Goal
