## Project Overview

Financial institutions need to understand investor behavior to design suitable investment products, personalize financial advice, and attract new clients.

In this project, I analyzed investment survey data and developed an interactive Power BI dashboard that reveals **who invests, what they invest in, why they invest, and the factors influencing their investment decisions.**

---

## Business Problem

WealthBridge Advisors wanted to grow its client base by answering critical business questions such as:

- Which investment products are most preferred?
- How do investment preferences differ across gender and age groups?
- What factors influence investment decisions?
- How do savings objectives vary by investment duration?
- What motivates investors to choose Mutual Funds?

The insights generated help support customer segmentation, product positioning, and data-driven marketing strategies.

## Key Insights

- **Mutual Funds** emerged as the most preferred investment avenue.
- Investors aged **26–30 years** represented the largest customer segment.
- **Investment Returns** were the strongest driver of investment decisions.
- **Retirement Planning** was the leading savings objective.
- Investors expecting **20–30% returns** showed the highest interest in Mutual Funds.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Position Mutual Funds as a flagship investment product.
- Develop targeted campaigns for investors aged 26–30 years.
- Provide investment education for younger, risk-conscious investors.
- Expand retirement-focused investment offerings.
